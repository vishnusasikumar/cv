About me

I am a senior mobile app developer with more than 10 years of experience designing, developing, and deploying high-performance iOS and Android applications. My career has spanned fintech, energy, retail, and emerging technology, giving me a unique ability to build apps that are not only technically robust but also aligned with real business impact.

On the technical side, I specialise in Swift, SwiftUI, and UIKit, with a strong focus on clean architecture (MVVM), scalable design patterns, and CI/CD automation. I’ve led the modernisation of legacy Objective-C/Swift codebases, implemented secure authentication and data management workflows, and delivered innovative solutions such as EV charging integrations, sustainability dashboards, and enterprise-grade financial apps. I bring deep experience in optimising app performance, implementing best practices in testing, and ensuring security compliance in sensitive domains.

What drives me is curiosity and continuous growth. I actively explore emerging technologies such as blockchain, composable SwiftUI architectures, and algorithmic trading, incorporating them into projects when they create real value. Equally important, I thrive in collaborative environments—mentoring junior developers, contributing to technical strategy, and ensuring cross-functional teams deliver intuitive, high-quality user experiences.

Whether modernising existing systems or crafting new applications from the ground up, I aim to deliver software that is reliable, user-centric, and future-ready.
