About me

I’m a Senior Mobile Software Engineer with 10+ years of experience delivering premium iOS applications across banking, utilities, retail and digital agencies. I collaborate closely with designers, product managers, QA engineers and clients to deliver polished, customer-facing mobile experiences.

I specialise in Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit and Objective-C, with expertise in Apple Pay, Apple Wallet, Wallet Extensions, Mastercard Digital Enablement Service (MDES), secure payment provisioning, CI/CD and scalable mobile architecture. I’ve led the modernisation of legacy Objective-C/Swift codebases, migrated apps from MVC to MVVM, implemented secure authentication and data workflows, and delivered solutions such as EV charging integrations, sustainability dashboards and enterprise-grade financial apps.

What drives me is curiosity and continuous growth. I actively explore emerging technologies such as composable SwiftUI architectures, TCA and offline-first design, bringing them into projects when they create real value. Equally important, I thrive in collaborative environments—mentoring developers, reviewing code, contributing to technical strategy, and helping cross-functional teams ship intuitive, high-quality experiences.

Whether modernising existing systems or crafting new applications from the ground up, I aim to deliver software that is reliable, user-centric, and future-ready.
